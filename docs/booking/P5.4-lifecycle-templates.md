# P5.4 — Lifecycle Notification Templates (approved-content drafts)

> Owner-provided copy (D-P5-4). **Approved-content drafts** — the **email** copy is final for Stage 2;
> the **SMS** copy is a draft to be **reconciled against the approved Twilio A2P campaign templates
> before Stage 4**. All transactional. Every SMS carries **STOP/HELP** where A2P requires. Reuse the
> existing `personalize.ts` merge vars — do **not** modify `personalize.ts`; the notify path supplies
> the context keys (§ "New merge vars" below).

## Ground rules (hold the line)
- **Email** fires on the **appointment-consent basis** (existing booking email opt-in). **SMS** fires
  **only** with the separate affirmative SMS consent (P5.3) — never inferred; every SMS has STOP.
- **Recap (#6) and no-show (#5) stay TRANSACTIONAL.** The moment either includes a product nudge,
  cross-sell, or referral ask it becomes **marketing** and needs separate marketing consent — NOT the
  appointment SMS consent. Keep them clean (thank-you / rebook only).
- `{{manage_url}}` / `{{reschedule_url}}` / `{{cancel_url}}` / `{{scheduling_link}}` render **absolute,
  signed** links (the D1/token fixes + `personalize.ts` URL-blocking-token contract already enforce
  this) — never relative, never a raw token. These are **blocking tokens**: an unresolved/relative one
  hard-blocks the send.

## Event → source_key → classifier mapping
`sendAppointmentNotice(appointmentId, event, channel)` loads the approved template by `source_key`:

| # | Event | Delivery-ledger `event` | Email source_key | SMS source_key | Fires |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirmation | `confirmation` | `appointment-confirmation` | `appointment-confirmation-sms` | on booking |
| 2 | Reminder | `reminder` (per offset) | `appointment-reminder-email` | `appointment-reminder-sms` | each configured offset (7d/36h/24h/1h…) |
| 3 | Rescheduled | `rescheduled` | `appointment-rescheduled` | `appointment-rescheduled-sms` | on reschedule (public + FSA) |
| 4 | Cancellation | `cancellation` | `appointment-cancellation` | `appointment-cancellation-sms` | on cancel |
| 5 | No-show follow-up | `no_show_followup` | `appointment-noshow` | `appointment-noshow-sms` | advisor marks no_show, **iff configured** |
| 6 | Recap / thank-you | `recap` | `appointment-recap` | `appointment-recap-sms` | on completed, **iff configured** |

> **Source-key reconciliation (implementation truth).** The **reminder email** key is
> `appointment-reminder-email` — the existing registry/`templates:build` key the reminder job
> already uses — NOT `appointment-reminder`; renaming it would churn `render_sha` and force a
> re-approval for zero benefit. Events 1/2/4/6 reuse the four already-registered appointment
> templates; Stage 2 **adds** only the two genuinely-missing components — `appointment-rescheduled`
> (`AppointmentRescheduled`) and `appointment-noshow` (`AppointmentNoShow`). All six email source
> keys are registry-registered so `templates:build` produces real, reviewable drafts.

No-show + recap are **opt-in** (a `booking_reminder_config`-style flag); off by default.

## Merge vars — enforced-token reconciliation (personalize.ts is NOT modified)
`personalize.ts` is authoritative (a380269) and **untouched**. Its BLOCKING tokens — `appointment_time`,
`meeting_details`, `reschedule_url`, `cancel_url`, `scheduling_link` (+ advisor/agency identity,
`unsubscribe_url`) — hard-block a send when unresolved, and its URL tokens hard-block a relative link.
The **email** templates deliberately ride only these **already-enforced** tokens:
- **Existing (supplied by `buildBookingContext` / injected by the send path):** `first_name`,
  `full_name`, `appointment_time` (the appointment's CURRENT time — after a reschedule the row's
  `starts_at` IS the new time, so the rescheduled notice's "New time" is `appointment_time`, a single
  enforced blocking token, rather than separate non-blocking `new_date`/`new_time`), `meeting_details`,
  `reschedule_url`, `cancel_url`, and `scheduling_link` (advisor-context default).
- **Deferred to Stage 4 (SMS only):** `manage_url` (the combined "Manage:" short link) is a **SMS**
  token; it is NOT a `personalize.ts` URL/blocking token, so no email relies on it and
  `personalize.ts` needs no change. Its absolute-signed guarantee is provided by the notify path
  (`manageUrls()` builds absolute signed links) when the SMS legs are wired in Stage 4.
- **Copy-spec convenience tokens** (`meeting_format`, `relative_when`, `new_date`, `new_time`,
  `next_steps`) were in the approved draft copy. Stage 2's email components convey the same
  transactional content through the design-system layout using the enforced tokens above, so these
  are **not** required for the email lifecycle. `relative_when` is available (pure `notify-events.ts`)
  for the SMS reminder copy in Stage 4; the rest are re-evaluated then.

## Stage 4 — SMS templates as CODE (reconciled to enforced tokens)
The SMS bodies below are the **approved-content drafts**; Stage 4 ships them as **code** in
`src/lib/booking/sms-templates.ts` (the SMS counterpart to the React email components), upserted to
`comm_templates` DRAFT rows by `npm run templates:build:sms` for the FSA to approve — the same
immutable-approval store + gate step-4 check every FSOS SMS uses, NOT a new store or advisor builder.
Reconciled to the **already-enforced** tokens (personalize.ts unchanged): `{{agency_name}}` (identity),
`{{first_name}}`, `{{appointment_time}}` (grounded full local time — the new time after a reschedule),
`{{reschedule_url}}` (absolute + signed), `{{scheduling_link}}` (absolute). **No `{{manage_url}}`** and
**no `{{practice_name}}`** — those draft tokens are not enforced; the enforced tokens carry the
absolute-signed guarantee. Every body keeps **STOP inline** (the dispatcher appends no SMS footer);
recap + no-show stay transactional. **Gating:** the SMS leg sends only when `sms_enabled=true`
(feature flag, Stage 4 default ON) **and** `SMS_A2P_APPROVED=true` (A2P go-live) **and** the affirmative
per-contact SMS consent resolves (Stage 3, `comm_contact_consents`) **and** the template is approved —
each independent. Proof: `tests/booking-sms-templates.test.mjs`.

## Approved copy (verbatim)

### 1. Confirmation
**Email — Subject:** `You're all set — {{appointment_date}}`
Hi {{first_name}}, — Your appointment with {{practice_name}} is confirmed. When: {{appointment_date}}
at {{appointment_time}} ({{timezone}}) · Format: {{meeting_format}} {{#meeting_details}}· Details:
{{meeting_details}}{{/meeting_details}}. Reschedule/cancel: {{reschedule_url}} · {{cancel_url}}.
Reply to this email with questions. — {{practice_name}}
**SMS:** `{{practice_name}}: You're confirmed for {{appointment_date}} at {{appointment_time}} {{timezone}}. Manage: {{manage_url}} Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.`

### 2. Reminder (per offset)
**Email — Subject:** `Reminder: your appointment is {{relative_when}}`
Hi {{first_name}}, — reminder your appointment with {{practice_name}} is coming up. When:
{{appointment_date}} at {{appointment_time}} ({{timezone}}) · Format: {{meeting_format}}
{{#meeting_details}}· Details: {{meeting_details}}{{/meeting_details}}. Reschedule/cancel:
{{reschedule_url}} · {{cancel_url}}. — {{practice_name}}
**SMS:** `{{practice_name}}: Reminder — your appointment is {{relative_when}} on {{appointment_date}} at {{appointment_time}} {{timezone}}. Manage: {{manage_url}} Reply STOP to opt out.`

### 3. Rescheduled
**Email — Subject:** `Your appointment has been rescheduled — {{new_date}}`
Hi {{first_name}}, — your appointment with {{practice_name}} has been moved. New time: {{new_date}}
at {{new_time}} ({{timezone}}) · Format: {{meeting_format}} {{#meeting_details}}· Details:
{{meeting_details}}{{/meeting_details}}. Your previous time is no longer reserved. Reschedule again or
cancel: {{reschedule_url}} · {{cancel_url}}. Reply with questions. — {{practice_name}}
**SMS:** `{{practice_name}}: Your appointment has been moved to {{new_date}} at {{new_time}} {{timezone}}. Manage: {{manage_url}} Reply STOP to opt out.`

### 4. Cancellation
**Email — Subject:** `Your appointment has been cancelled`
Hi {{first_name}}, — your appointment with {{practice_name}} on {{appointment_date}} at
{{appointment_time}} ({{timezone}}) has been cancelled. Book again: {{scheduling_link}}. Reply if this
was a mistake. — {{practice_name}}
**SMS:** `{{practice_name}}: Your appointment on {{appointment_date}} at {{appointment_time}} {{timezone}} has been cancelled. Rebook: {{scheduling_link}} Reply STOP to opt out.`

### 5. No-show follow-up (advisor-marked, iff configured — TRANSACTIONAL)
**Email — Subject:** `Sorry we missed you — let's reschedule`
Hi {{first_name}}, — we missed you at your appointment with {{practice_name}} on {{appointment_date}}.
Book a new time: {{scheduling_link}}. — {{practice_name}}
**SMS:** `{{practice_name}}: Sorry we missed you {{appointment_date}}. Rebook anytime: {{scheduling_link}} Reply STOP to opt out.`

### 6. Recap / thank-you (on completion, iff configured — TRANSACTIONAL, no promo)
**Email — Subject:** `Thanks for meeting with {{practice_name}}`
Hi {{first_name}}, — thank you for meeting with {{practice_name}} today. {{#next_steps}}Next steps:
{{next_steps}}{{/next_steps}} Reply with any questions. — {{practice_name}}
**SMS:** `{{practice_name}}: Thanks for meeting with us today, {{first_name}}. Questions? Just reply. Reply STOP to opt out.`

## D-P5-5 — consent evidence store: **audit-record** (recommended, adopted unless owner overrides)
The append-only, tamper-locked `audit_log` `consent.captured` row carries the full structured evidence
(P5.3 §2a) — TCPA-defensible with **no new table**. A dedicated `booking_sms_consent_events` table
(workshop_consent_events precedent) remains available if a first-class structured store is later
wanted, but is not needed for defensibility.
